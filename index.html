About

I'm a Laravel and PHP full-stack engineer with 6 years of experience. At CoreProc I've shipped features across 12 client applications and written 2,000+ commits.

Most of what I build is business software: approval and reconciliation workflows, sales and wholesaler portals, subscription billing, and the REST APIs and integrations that connect them to Salesforce, PayPal, PayMaya, Google SSO and SFTP data feeds. I work across the stack, from the MySQL schema and Laravel services to Vue, Inertia.js and Livewire screens.

I lead features from requirement to production. I write design specs and test plans before coding, debug and fix production incidents, and solve performance problems at the query and queue level, so applications stay fast and maintainable as their data grows.

I also own system design on my projects, hold the code-quality bar through pull request reviews, and onboard new developers to the codebase and team conventions.
